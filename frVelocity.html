Fread Peak Velocity Information

The Fread velocity equation was provided by Danny Fread at a FLDWAV workshop held at OHD in November 2006.

Flood wave velocity associated with a dam breach is computed as follows:

$$c = \left(\frac{0.005}{n}\right) \cdot H^{2/3} \cdot S^{1/2}$$

Where:

  • \(c\) = Wave Speed (mi/hr)
  • \(n\) = Manning's n
  • \(H\) = Breach Head (ft) - defined as Depth of Water at time of Breaching
  • \(S\) = Channel Bottom Slope (ft/mi)

Suggested Manning's "n" Values

A brief sample of possible Manning's "n" values follow, extracted from Chow, V.T., "Open Channel Hydraulics", McGraw-Hill, 1959:

Main Channels

Descriptionn Value
Clean, straight channel with no rifts or pools0.030
Clean, winding, some pools and shoals0.040
Clean, winding, with stones0.050
Sluggish reaches, weedy, deep pools0.070
Very weedy reaches, deep pools, floodways with heavy timber and underbrush0.100

Flood Plains

Descriptionn Value
Pasture, no brush0.030
Cultivated areas (no crops)0.030
Cultivated areas (mature field crops)0.040
Scattered brush with heavy weeds0.050
Light brush and trees (winter)0.050
Light brush and trees (summer)0.060
Medium to dense brush (winter)0.070

Forest

Descriptionn Value
Dense willows, summer0.150
Cleared land with tree stumps, no sprouts0.040
Cleared land with tree stumps, heavy sprouts0.060
Heavy timber stand, few downed trees, flood stage below branches0.100
Heavy timber stand, few downed trees, flood stage reaching branches0.120
