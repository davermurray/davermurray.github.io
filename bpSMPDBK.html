SMPDBK Breach Width Information

The NWS Simplified Dam-Break Model uses default "Rules of Thumb" values for breach width based upon the type of dam. They are as follows:

  • Earthen Dam: \(B = 3 \times H\)
  • Concrete Gravity Dam: \(B = 5 \times H\)
  • Concrete Arch Dam: \(B = 0.9 \times W\)

Where:

  • \(B\) = Breach Width (ft)
  • \(H\) = Breach Head (ft) - defined as Depth of Water at time of Breach
  • \(W\) = Maximum Tailwater Width (Crest Length) of Dam (ft)

SMPDBK Breach Time Information

The NWS Simplified Dam-Break Model uses default "Rules of Thumb" values for breach time based upon the type of dam. They are as follows:

  • Earthen Dam: \(T = H / 10\)
  • Concrete Gravity Dam: \(T = H / 40\)
  • Concrete Arch Dam: \(T = H / 50\)

Where:

  • \(T\) = Breach Time (min)
  • \(H\) = Breach Head (ft) - defined as Depth of Water at time of Breaching
Additional information can be found in the document "The NWS Simplified Dam-Break Flood forecasting model" by Wetmore J. and Fread, D.; revised in 1991 by Fread, D., Lewis, Janice and Wiele, S.
