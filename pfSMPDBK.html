SMPDBK Peak Outflow Information

Peak outflow at the dam is computed with the falling head broad-crested weir flow equation used by the Simplified Dam Break Model (SMPDBK).

$$Q = 3.1B \cdot \left[ \frac{C}{(t/60) + (C/\sqrt{H})} \right]^3$$

Where the constant \(C\) is:

$$C = 23.4 \cdot \left( \frac{S}{B} \right)$$

And:

  • \(Q\) = Peak Outflow (cfs)
  • \(S\) = Reservoir Surface Area (acre)
  • \(t\) = Breach Time (min)
  • \(B\) = Average Breach Width (ft)
  • \(H\) = Breach Head (ft) - defined as Depth of Water at time of Breaching

Default Values

Default values used by the SMPDBK can be estimated as follows:

  • Reservoir Surface Area: 2 × Reservoir Volume / dam height
  • Breach Head (H): Dam crest elevation − the invert (bottom) elevation of the first (tailwater) cross section
  • Breach Width: 3 × height of earthen dam OR 5 × height of gravity dam OR 90% height of concrete arch dam
  • Breach Time: near 0 for concrete arch dam OR Breach Head (H) / 3 for earthen dams
